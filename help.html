Common questions

When will my order ship?

Most orders ship within 1 business day. You’ll get an email with tracking as soon as it leaves our warehouse.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders over $75. Otherwise, standard shipping is $6.95.

Can I change or cancel my order?

We can usually change or cancel an order within an hour of placing it. Chat with us as soon as possible.

How do I return something?

Chat with Fern to start a return. You’ll get a prepaid label by email. See returns and exchanges for details.

How do I find my size?

Each product page lists fit notes. Our gear specialists can also help you choose on chat.

Do you repair gear?

Yes. We repair zippers, seams, and buckles on anything we sell, for life. Chat with us to arrange a repair.
