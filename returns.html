Returns and exchanges

Return unused gear within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Returns are always free.

  1. Start your return

    Chat with Fern and have your order number ready. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Pack it up

    We email you a prepaid label. Use any box, as long as it’s sealed.

  3. Drop it off

    Take it to any UPS location. Refunds post within 5 business days of arrival.

Start a return now

Fern checks whether your order qualifies and sends your label right away. Need something else? Fern connects you with our team.

What you can return

Unused items in their original condition, with tags attached, within 60 days of delivery.

Used or older gear

If something failed on the trail, our warranty and repair team can help, even after 60 days. Chat with us and we’ll take a look.

Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange is to return the original item and place a new order. Fern can hold your size while the return is on its way.
